# Events and meetings

## Event prep

1. Confirm the goal: pipeline, partnerships, hiring, learning, or visibility,
   and how many conversations the user can realistically have.
2. Research attendees, speakers, and sponsors with `exa_agent_run` (list-style
   runs through `lead-generation`) or `web_search` for a quick check. Cite
   sources; mark anything unverified.
3. Produce a short plan in a workspace file: priority targets, one-line reason
   to talk to each, an opener, and a follow-up plan. Do not invent attendees.
4. Track targets in the pipeline home: the Tempo connection for company
   pipeline, or a Sheet for a project-only list (see `outreach-and-messaging`).

## Meeting briefs

Before a meeting, gather what the user has: the Calendar and Gmail
connections for context, Tempo for account history, then `company-research` if
more is needed. Write a one-page brief: who, their company, history, what the
user wants out of it, questions to ask, and risks. Only use facts you found;
label gaps.

## Follow-ups

After a meeting, turn the user's notes into: a summary, decisions, owners, and
next steps with dates they stated. Draft the follow-up email as a draft only;
sending follows the confirmation rules in `outreach-and-messaging`. Record the
activity in Tempo (with approval) or in the Sheet.

## Rules

- Do not book, accept, decline, or send anything on the user's behalf without
  explicit confirmation.
- Do not invent meeting details, commitments, or quotes. Quote the user's notes.
